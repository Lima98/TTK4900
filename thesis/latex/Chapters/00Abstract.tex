The use of procedurally content has grown widespread in many different fields in recent years from video games to urban planning. However procedural music has not been covered as comprehensively as other domains. Procedural music generation present a unique challenge due to the highly subjective nature of what is considered good music.

This thesis takes well known musical concepts and conceptualises them as constraint in a procedural generation system. The system was developed through three iterations where the final iteration was assisted by artificial intelligence in order to asses its usefulness as a development tool.

The resulting system is able to generate melodies and harmonies adhering to theoretical musical principles and can easily be shaped by the user to weigh different musical concepts to personal preference.
