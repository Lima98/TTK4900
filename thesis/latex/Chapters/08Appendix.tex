This appendix gathers a broader selection of outputs from the third iteration than could reasonably be included in the main discussion. The examples are organized by purpose: first a survey across all twelve major keys, then examples showing formal control, explicit harmony, longer spans, and automatic SATB-style harmonization.

\section*{Explicit harmonic control}\label{sec:appendix-explicit}
The third iteration also allows the harmonic progression to be specified directly from the command line. These two examples use a new explicit progression in A major that includes modal mixture through both the borrowed minor subdominant and a borrowed flat seventh chord. The first shows the melody on its own, while the second shows the same harmonic plan realized as a harmonized texture.

\begin{example}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{examples/appendix/iter3_explicit_mixture_melody_a.cropped.pdf}
  \caption[Explicit-harmony melody in A major with modal mixture.]{Melody generated in A major from an explicitly specified harmonic progression that includes the borrowed \texttt{iv} and \texttt{bVII} chords. \examplelink}
  \label{fig:c-explicit-mixture-melody-a}
\end{example}

\begin{example}[H]
